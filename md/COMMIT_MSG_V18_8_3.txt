V18.8.3: index.ts barrel deleted + 2 straggler rateLimitMs removals

V18.8.3 — the index.ts barrel cleanup. The spec called for
"src/services/api/index.ts is now ~5 lines (was 36)" but
the cleaner outcome is to **delete** the barrel: no consumer
in src/ or __tests__/ imports from it, no config file
references it, and the V18 ideal is "1 import per consumer
directly from the adapter" (the barrel adds a layer of
indirection that's unused).

What was actually done:

  1. `git rm src/services/api/index.ts` — 67 lines deleted.
     The barrel was already partially cleaned in V18.3.4
     (5 simple-service re-exports removed) and V18.4.3
     (3 paginated re-exports removed) and V18.5 (IA
     re-export updated). The remaining content was the
     selective type-only re-exports for the TVMaze /
     MusicBrainz / LibriVox / Jamendo / Audius services,
     plus the `export *` lines for the now-clean
     radioBrowser / IPTV / IA adapters. With no consumer
     using the barrel, the cleanest end-state is no barrel
     at all.

  2. Two `rateLimitMs: 200,` stragglers removed (V18.8.1
     missed them because they weren't in the *Adapter.ts
     files called out by the PowerShell regex; they were
     in local ApiConfig object literals inside
     weatherAdapter and jamendoAdapter):
       - `src/services/api/weatherAdapter.ts:234` (FORECAST_CONFIG)
       - `src/services/api/weatherAdapter.ts:239` (GEOCODING_CONFIG)
       - `src/services/api/jamendoAdapter.ts:24` (JAMENDO_CONFIG)

     Without these, tsc would have failed with
     "Object literal may only specify known properties,
     and 'rateLimitMs' does not exist in type 'ApiConfig'."

The cascade on the spec:

  - Spec said index.ts goes from 36 → ~5 lines. Actual:
    deleted entirely (-67 lines).
  - The spec's example (`export {default as weatherAdapter}
    from './weatherAdapter'`) was the V18.0 default-export
    pattern; the V18.1 type-contract revision replaced
    that with selective named exports, which is what the
    barrel had when no consumer wanted it.
