"V18.2 Lessons Learned" section added at the end of
Phase 8 — the 7-point design notes that will propagate
to Waves 3-9. Key takeaways:

1. Convertor naming: xxxResultFromRaw / xxxResultsFromRaw.
2. Service functions are 2 lines: apiFetch + convertor.
3. Sequential cascade fits in 1 useApiQuery; parallel
   fan-out (V18.6) needs useQueries.
4. The store-mirror useEffect is the only V18<->V17 seam.
5. useApiQuery tests: use toHaveBeenCalled, not
   toHaveBeenCalledTimes(1) — TanStack refetches in tests.
6. No node -e require(...) in V18; tsc + jest only.
7. 1 useApiQuery per service method (cascade as queryFn).
