Wave 11 legacy TabView cleanup, phase 5 of 6.

Before (3 tabs):
  - search   (TVMaze /search/shows?q=…)
  - today    (TVMaze /schedule, date-bucketed)
  - browse   (TVMaze /shows, paginated)

After (1 active stream + rail):

    to be a hero rail (the data hook is already in place).

    and the rail pattern gives the same content without
    requiring a tab switch.

    todayRail. One source-of-truth `active` selector with

    `react-native-tab-view` under src/).
  - types/index.ts      — already clean (no `ShowTab` /
    `ShowScopeState` to remove).

UX decisions (V18.11.5 inline):
  - "Today" tab kept as a rail (not dropped) — it surfaces
    relevant, time-sensitive content and a single-chip + rail
    is the modern, lower-friction pattern.
  - Search is the primary; Browse is the empty-state default.
  - The chip is a "source" hint only — the search term
    always wins when set.
