V18.3.4: delete the 5 shim files + update the index.ts barrel

Wave 3 closeout. The 5 deprecated re-export shim files
(audiusService.ts, jamendoService.ts, librivoxService.ts,
musicbrainzService.ts, tvmazeService.ts) are no longer
needed — every consumer hook has migrated to import from
the corresponding adapter directly. The shims served
their purpose (no app breakage during the gradual hook
migration); now that all 10 of 11 consumer hooks are on
the adapters, the shims are pure indirection.

Also updates two files that still imported from the shims:
  - src/services/api/index.ts (the barrel export)
  - src/services/api/searchAggregator.ts (the cross-service
    aggregator)
  - src/screens/Search/hooks/useAggregatedSearch.ts
    (the one consumer that hadn't migrated; it's the
    V18.6-deferred work but the import was trivial)
