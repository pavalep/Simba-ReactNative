V20.9 — Full lift of per-screen types/index.ts (3× duplicate + Home union)

The 3 per-screen copies of `types/index.ts` at
  src/screens/MusicScreen/types/index.ts
  src/screens/MoviesScreen/types/index.ts
  src/screens/PodcastsScreen/types/index.ts
each defined 8 types locally (SectionRouteKey, SectionRouteParams,
SectionOptionGroupId, OptionItem, OptionGroup, SectionOptionsMerged,
SectionRenderContext, SectionBrowseConfig). All 3 copies were
structurally identical for 7 of the 8 types, but the
SectionRenderContext had diverged — PodcastsScreen was a 3-field
KISS version (with an explicit comment explaining why), and
MusicScreen / MoviesScreen still carried 4 legacy fields
(`activeChips`, `refreshing`, `onRetry`, `routeParams`) that no
v10 content reads.

The Home screen also had its own `SectionRouteKey` (a union of
all 9 Discover destinations) at
`src/screens/Home/components/browseAllRouteKey.ts` — the union
is the canonical source; each per-screen literal is structurally
assignable to it.

The lift:

  1. New `src/screens/_shared/sectionRoute.ts` — the union
     `SectionRouteKey` (all 9 Home destinations) + `SectionRouteParams`.
  2. New `src/screens/_shared/sectionRenderContext.ts` — the shared
     `SectionRenderContext` interface. The 4 legacy fields are typed
     as `?` (optional) so all 3 BrowseLayouts continue to type-check
     — Music/Movies pass 7 fields, Podcasts passes 3. A future KISS
     pass (V20.14) could drop the optional fields entirely and
     refactor Music/Movies BrowseLayout to also pass just the 3
     required fields.
  3. `src/screens/_shared/sectionBrowseConfig.ts` is extended with
     the full `SectionBrowseConfig` interface (alongside the V20.7
     `SectionBrowseConfigSearch` and V20.8 `SectionBrowseConfigOptions`
     subsets the shared hooks use).
  4. `src/screens/_shared/sectionOptions.ts` (added in V20.8)
     already carries the option types. No change.
  5. The 3 per-screen `types/index.ts` are now thin re-exports
     from the shared location. Every existing
     `import ... from '../types'` call site (in BrowseLayout,
     Content, browseConfig.ts, index.tsx) keeps working unchanged.
  6. `src/screens/Home/components/browseAllRouteKey.ts` becomes a
     re-export of the shared `SectionRouteKey`. The 2 call sites
     (`BrowseAllShelf.tsx` and the per-screen `types/index.ts`)
     keep working unchanged.

Net line count: -180 lines of duplication + ~60 lines of shared
modules (1 new file for route types, 1 new file for render
context, +1 type in sectionBrowseConfig.ts). 4 consumer-facing
files are now 1-line re-exports.

Verification:
  - `npx tsc --noEmit` clean.
  - `npx jest --forceExit` clean: 9 suites, 106 passed, 1 todo.
