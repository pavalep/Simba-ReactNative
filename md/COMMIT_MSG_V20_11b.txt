V20.11b — IPTV logo→image rename (3 screen call sites + 1 test)

The `IPTVChannelResult` domain type had a `logo` field
(inherited from the IPTV-org API's wire shape). Every
screen consumer had to rename it to `image` for the row
component to render. The IPTV adapter is a proper V18-ideal
adapter with `IPTVChannelRaw` + `channelResultFromRaw`, but
the convertor returned `logo` and the screen layer carried
the rename. This commit normalizes the domain type on `image`
to match the project's UI convention (and to match
`RadioStationResult.image` from V20.11a).

Changes:
  1. `src/types/api.ts` — `IPTVChannelResult.logo: string` is
     renamed to `image: string`.
  2. `src/services/api/iptvAdapter.ts` —
     `channelResultFromRaw` now returns `image: raw.logo || ''`
     (the `Raw` shape still uses the wire's `logo` key; the
     convertor is the boundary that does the rename).
  3. `src/screens/LiveTVScreenNew/components/ChannelCard.tsx` —
     the `toRow` function's `image: channel.logo || ''` becomes
     `image: channel.image || ''`. The 3 other field reads
     (id, name, url) are unchanged.
  4. `src/screens/LiveTVScreenNew/hooks/useLiveTVBrowser.ts`
     line 185 — `image: channel.logo || ''` becomes
     `image: channel.image || ''` (the favorites-store push).
  5. `src/screens/Search/components/RemoteResults.tsx` line 313
     — `imageUrl={item.logo}` becomes `imageUrl={item.image}`
     (the Search-screen IPTV row).
  6. `__tests__/paginatedAdapters.test.ts` — the
     `channelResultFromRaw maps a complete channel` test's
     expected object is updated from `logo` to `image` to
     match the new domain type.

Net result: the screen layer no longer carries a redundant
field rename, and `IPTVChannelResult.image` is now consistent
with `RadioStationResult.image` (and the `useLiveFavoritesStore`
`image` field that both adapters write to).

Verification:
  - `npx tsc --noEmit` clean.
  - `npx jest --forceExit` clean: 9 suites, 106 passed, 1 todo.
