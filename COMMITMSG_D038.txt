D-038 (consumer-side binary override — centerpiece of this commit)
==================================================================
v1.5.7 shipped with mpv-android 2026-08-11 which regressed video output
on x86_64 GFXSTREAM emulators and several Mali drivers — mpv decodes
frames fine, but vo=gpu's EGL bootstrap fails before any pixel reaches
the SurfaceView. The fix is to ship the pre-mpv-android-2026-08-11
libmpv.so bundle at the consumer side (where it survives across gradle
cleans) and use packagingOptions excludes to force AGP's native-lib
merge to pick the consumer's nested copies over the AAR's flat
contribution.

Why the lib + consumer split
============================
The lib publishes at v1.5.7's stock mpv bundle (no binary blobs in the
npm tarball). The consumer adds the binary override at the boundary.
This keeps the lib portable for projects that DON'T need the override
(ARM-only devices where the new mpv-android 2026-08-11 might be fine),
while letting the SIMBA consumer carry the override forward until the
mpv-android team ships a fix.

If you fork this repo or build on a fresh machine, the jniLibs/
files are checked in so `./gradlew :app:assembleDebug` Just Works
without needing the archive at tools/native-backups/ to exist.

Co-carries: D-036 defensive cleanup (DefaultControls rgba → transparent,
pointerEvents='box-none'). The rgba(0,0,0,0.001) hack has been there
since v1.5.0 era and was NOT the cause of the v1.5.7 video regression —
but it's still confusing code that obscures intent. Replaced with the
actual goal: a fully transparent fill View that doesn't intercept
taps on the controls.
