Gate audio chrome on streamType so video launches never show the audio bar

A video launch also writes the nowPlaying store, and AudioChrome gated
only on `nowPlaying != null`. Opening any movie therefore rendered the
audio mini bar on top of the video window — the same class of bug the
in-app audio player was built to eliminate.

`streamType` records the 'video' | 'audio' the bridge was actually asked
for, written by the launch seam from `bridgeOpts.type` — the single
place every launch funnels through. It is deliberately distinct from
`mediaLane` (a display lane, absent at many launch sites) and from
`type` (a catalogue MediaKind). Gating on `mediaLane` would have hidden
the bar for every site that passes a stream type but no lane.

Three new tests pin the lane: video launch renders nothing, audio launch
renders, and toggling between them re-gates without remount.
Mutation-checked: 12 pass, dropping the gate fails 1.

Gates: tsc 0, eslint 0, jest 0 (80 suites / 1001 passed / 1002).
