V17 Phase 87: V17 release v1.6.0 (consumer-only)

V17 is shipped. The 13 phases of state migration are done:
- 11 persisted zustand stores replace the redux slice
  hierarchy (1:1 persist-key mapping, version: 1 on each)
- 2 transient stores (session, plus the pre-existing auth
  sessionExpiresAt) keep the no-persist patterns
- `<Provider>` + `<PersistGate>` are out of `App.tsx`
- `@reduxjs/toolkit` + `react-redux` + `redux-persist` +
  `redux-logger` are out of `package.json`
- `src/store/` is gone

Tagged `v17.0.0` (consumer-only — `package.json` is
`"private": true`, so there's no npm publish; the release
is a git tag + this changelog + the final QA report).

Final QA report at `md/SIMBA_PLAYER_MODULE_V17_FINAL_QA_REPORT.md`
covers the per-phase outcomes, the public-surface delta, the
V16 out-of-scope fixes that V17 picked up, and the V18+
candidates.
